# 笠地蔵 (Kasa Jizō — Ông lão bán nón rơm & 6 tượng Địa Tạng) — Art Direction & Image Prompts

All assets target **16-bit SNES / GBA warm Japanese picture-book pixel art** locked to the repository's `jp_story_master` palette with crisp dark espresso outlines (`#241712`).

- **Aspect ratio for backgrounds:** `16:10` (processed down to `320×200`, `dither: 18`, zero baked text, zero characters inside backgrounds).
- **Character bust sheets (`ojiisan`, `obaasan`):** 4-column horizontal strip on solid chroma-green `#00FF00` background (`[0] neutral`, `[1] talking open mouth`, `[2] blink eyes closed`, `[3] warm smile talking`). Eyes and head pose stay rock-steady across frames 0, 1, 3 so `frame_regions` locks `eye_diff == 0`.
- **Walk cycle (`ojiisan_walk`):** 8-frame horizontal strip facing RIGHT on solid `#00FF00` background (frames 0–5 gentle winter walking cycle, frames 6–7 standing idle + blink).
- **Pre-crafted pixel items (already in `art-src/kasa-jizo/`):** `raw_item_straw_hat.png`, `raw_item_tenugui.png`, `raw_item_mochi_gift.png`, `raw_item_rice_bale.png`.
